Traveler briefing

What it is

A recurring full-ship music week: Signature Cruise Experiences charters Celebrity Summit so smooth-jazz headliners and guests own the vessel for seven nights—Caribbean winter loops plus a West Coast reposition—not a cabin block on a public sailing.

Who goes / who should not

Go if you want nonstop smooth-jazz stages, sail-away pool parties, and a house super-band with repeat cruisers. Skip if you want quiet mainstream cruising, flexible last-minute cabins, or a different franchise’s lineup.

The door

No public dress-code page found. Door is an SCE reservation plus required travel documents to board the chartered Celebrity Summit—you cannot walk on with a random Celebrity ticket.

Why the week exists

Winter sailings exist so the genre can run a floating festival at sea; next named weeks are the Sep 2026 West Coast ‘26.3 and the Jan–Feb 2027 Tampa loops, all fully reserved with waitlists.

They rented: Celebrity Summit — full-ship charter for the week (not a cabin on a public Celebrity sailing)
